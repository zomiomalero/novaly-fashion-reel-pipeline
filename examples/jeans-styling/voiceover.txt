The easiest way to make jeans look intentional is balance. Wide jeans usually need a cleaner top. Slim jeans feel better with something relaxed. Straight cuts work with tailoring. Barrel jeans look sharp with simple fitted layers. Bootcut denim deserves an elegant neckline. And with pale denim, soft color keeps everything fresh. The trick isn't buying more jeans. It's changing the shape of what you wear above them.
